# x19 2022年高考 浙江卷（1月） Continuation Writing 心理学课上的搭档：第 1 屏片头（GAOKAO · WRITING · CONTINUATION、标题、 试卷 · 篇目、体裁 记叙文 · 友谊与成长、全文 357 词 · 逐句精读），之后每句一屏，共 19 句；长句分屏（页脚 PART m/n）。

screen02  第 1 句
  英文：When Dr. Henderson was assigning (指定) project mates for his psychology class, I secretly hoped he would pair me with my best friend or at least a classmate I could have some fun with.
  中文：亨德森博士给他的心理学课指定项目搭档时，我暗暗希望他把我和最好的朋友分在一组，至少也分给我一个能一起玩得开心的同学。
  生词：assign v. 指定；分派；project n. 项目；课题；mate n. 伙伴；搭档；psychology n. 心理学；secretly adv. 暗地里；pair…with 使……与……配对；at least 至少；classmate n. 同学；have fun with 和……玩得开心

screen03  第 2 句
  英文：Above all, I hoped he wouldn’t assign me to work with the fiercely competitive, extremely serious fellow who always wore dark clothes and apparently had a personality to match.
  中文：最重要的是，我希望他别安排我和那个家伙一起做——他好胜心极强、极其严肃，总穿深色衣服，看样子性格也和衣服一样阴沉。
  生词：above all 最重要的是；assign v. 指派；fiercely adv. 极度地；猛烈地；competitive adj. 好胜的；竞争的；extremely adv. 极其；fellow n. 家伙；小伙子；apparently adv. 看样子；显然；personality n. 性格；个性；to match 与之相配的

screen04  第 3 句
  英文：As fate (命运) would have it, Dr. Henderson very deliberately matched everyone in class and announced that I would be working with the one person in class I wanted to avoid.
  中文：偏偏天意弄人，亨德森博士十分用心地给班上每个人配好了对，还宣布我将和班上我唯一想躲开的那个人合作。
  生词：as fate would have it 偏偏；无巧不成书；deliberately adv. 刻意地；从容审慎地；match v. 使配对；announce v. 宣布；the one person 唯一的那个人；avoid v. 避开

screen05  第 4 句
  英文：I went up to my new teammate and introduced myself.
  中文：我走到新搭档跟前，作了自我介绍。
  生词：go up to 走近；teammate n. 队友；搭档；introduce oneself 作自我介绍

screen06  第 5 句
  英文：He looked at me as though I weren’t there.
  中文：他看着我，就好像我不存在一样。
  生词：as though 好像；仿佛

screen07  第 6 句
  英文：I felt he treated me as though I would hold him back and probably make him fail to get an A in the course.
  中文：我觉得他对待我的样子，仿佛我会拖他后腿，没准还会害他这门课拿不到A。
  生词：treat v. 对待；as though 好像；仿佛；hold…back 阻碍；拖……后腿；probably adv. 很可能；fail to 未能；course n. 课程

screen08  第 7 句
  英文：He wasn’t mean or abusive; he just gave me the impression he could do whatever project we dreamed up better if he did it alone.
  中文：他并不刻薄，也没有恶语相向；他只是给我一种印象：不管我们想出什么项目，他自己一个人做都会做得更好。
  生词：mean adj. 刻薄的；abusive adj. 辱骂的；恶语伤人的；give sb the impression 给某人……的印象；whatever det. 无论什么；project n. 项目；dream up 想出；构想出；alone adv. 独自

screen09  第 8 句
  英文：Needless to say, I didn’t look forward to an entire term of being brushed off, but I tried to make the best of it and didn’t say anything for fear that I would make things worse.
  中文：不用说，我可不盼着整整一个学期都被人冷落，但我还是尽量随遇而安，什么也没说，生怕把事情弄得更糟。
  生词：needless to say 不用说；look forward to 盼望；entire adj. 整个的；term n. 学期；brush off 对……不理睬；冷落；make the best of 尽力应付（不利处境）；for fear that 生怕；以免

screen10  第 9 句
  英文：The project required each team to develop a hypothesis (假说), set up an experiment to test the hypothesis, do the statistical analysis and present the findings.
  中文：这个项目要求每个小组提出一个假说，设计实验来检验它，做统计分析，并展示研究结果。
  生词：project n. 项目；require v. 要求；develop v. 提出；形成；hypothesis n. 假说；set up 设立；安排；experiment n. 实验；test v. 检验；statistical adj. 统计的；analysis n. 分析；present v. 展示；陈述；finding n. 研究结果

screen11  第 10 句
  英文：Whatever grade the team received would be shared by both students.
  中文：小组得到的分数不论高低，都由两名学生共享。
  生词：whatever det. 无论什么；grade n. 分数；成绩；receive v. 得到；share v. 共享

screen12  第 11 句
  英文：When my teammate and I met to discuss our project, I was uneasy.
  中文：我和搭档碰面讨论项目时，心里很不安。
  生词：teammate n. 队友；搭档；discuss v. 讨论；project n. 项目；uneasy adj. 不安的

screen13  第 12 句
  英文：Here was this challenging student who had a reputation for single-mindedness and good grades—the exact opposite of me.
  中文：眼前就是这个难对付的同学，他出了名的一心一意、成绩优秀——和我恰恰相反。
  生词：here was （倒装）眼前就是；challenging adj. 难对付的；有挑战性的；have a reputation for 以……出名；single-mindedness n. 一心一意；专心致志；grade n. 分数；成绩；exact adj. 恰好的；确切的；opposite n. 对立面；相反的人

screen14  第 13 句
  英文：I was outmatched.
  中文：我根本不是他的对手。
  生词：outmatch v. 胜过；比……强（be outmatched 不是对手）

screen15  第 14 句
  英文：I actually wanted to drop the class at one point, but stopped short because I didn’t want to give him the satisfaction of my chickening out.
  中文：有一阵子我真想退掉这门课，但最后还是忍住了，因为我不想让他看到我临阵退缩而暗自得意。
  生词：actually adv. 真的；实际上；drop the class 退课；at one point 有一阵子；一度；stop short 突然停住；忍住没做；give sb the satisfaction of 让某人因……而得意；chicken out 临阵退缩

screen16  第 15 句
  英文：I decided to stick to it no matter what.
  中文：我下定决心，无论如何都要坚持下去。
  生词：stick to it 坚持下去；no matter what 无论如何

screen17  第 16 句
  英文：After long discussions we somehow agreed to do a study on the psychological well-being of teenagers.
  中文：讨论了很久之后，我们总算同意做一项关于青少年心理健康的研究。
  生词：discussion n. 讨论；somehow adv. 不知怎么地；总算；agree to 同意（做）；do a study on 就……做研究；psychological adj. 心理的；well-being n. 健康；幸福；teenager n. 青少年

screen18  第 17 句
  英文：I wasn’t sure what it meant exactly, but at least we had a topic.
  中文：我不太清楚它确切指什么，但至少我们有了题目。
  生词：exactly adv. 确切地；at least 至少；topic n. 题目；话题

screen19  第 18 句
  英文：We started to meet regularly to draw up our plans.
  中文：我们开始定期碰面，拟订计划。
  生词：regularly adv. 定期地；draw up 拟订；起草

  生词：get word 得到消息；be admitted to hospital 住院；serious adj. 严重的；disease n. 疾病
