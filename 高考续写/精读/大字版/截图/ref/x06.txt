# x06 2025年高考 新课标II卷 Continuation Writing 留学生名字发音的烦恼：第 1 屏片头（GAOKAO · WRITING · CONTINUATION、标题、 试卷 · 篇目、体裁 记叙文 · 文化与身份认同、全文 299 词 · 逐句精读），之后每句一屏，共 20 句；长句分屏（页脚 PART m/n）。

screen02  第 1 句
  英文：“What is your name?” is a question most frequently asked when people meet for the first time.
  中文：“你叫什么名字？”是人们初次见面时最常问的问题。
  生词：frequently adv. 频繁地；经常；for the first time 第一次；初次

screen03  第 2 句
  英文：But for me, it was the first challenge I encountered as an international student in Ireland.
  中文：可对我来说，这却是我作为留学生在爱尔兰遇到的第一个挑战。
  生词：challenge n. 挑战；难题；encounter v. 遇到；碰到；international student 国际学生；留学生；Ireland n. 爱尔兰

screen04  第 3 句
  英文：The pronunciation system of the Chinese language is quite different from that of English.
  中文：汉语的发音体系和英语的大不相同。
  生词：pronunciation n. 发音；system n. 体系；系统；be different from 与……不同；that of ……的那个（代替前面的名词）

screen05  第 4 句
  英文：For native speakers of English, some Chinese words are rather difficult to pronounce.
  中文：对以英语为母语的人来说，有些汉语词语相当难念。
  生词：native speaker 说母语的人；rather adv. 相当；颇；pronounce v. 发……的音；念

screen06  第 5 句
  英文：My given name Qiuyu (秋雨), for instance, happened to be a great challenge for many of them.
  中文：比如，我的名字“秋雨”对他们中的许多人来说就恰好是个大难题。
  生词：given name 名字（不含姓）；for instance 例如；happen to 碰巧；恰好；challenge n. 挑战；难题

screen07  第 6 句
  英文：Every time I gave a self-introduction, I had to explain how to pronounce my name at least five times, yet they still could not say it the way I did.
  中文：每次做自我介绍，我都得至少解释五遍我的名字怎么念，可他们还是念不成我念的那样。
  生词：every time 每次；self-introduction n. 自我介绍；explain v. 解释；pronounce v. 发……的音；at least 至少；yet conj. 可是；然而；the way I did 像我那样

screen08  第 7 句
  英文：Once in a lecture, the professor tried repeating my name after me over and over in front of thirty classmates.
  中文：有一次上课，教授当着三十个同学的面，跟着我一遍又一遍地念我的名字。
  生词：lecture n. 讲座；（大学的）讲课；professor n. 教授；repeat v. 重复；跟着说；over and over 一遍又一遍；in front of 在……面前；classmate n. 同学

screen09  第 8 句
  英文：I really did not know whether I should continue correcting him or simply drop the matter.
  中文：我真不知道是该继续纠正他，还是干脆就此作罢。
  生词：whether conj. 是否；continue v. 继续；correct v. 纠正；simply adv. 干脆；索性；drop the matter 不再提这件事；作罢

screen10  第 9 句
  英文：I feared that my classmates might grow tired of my efforts or even lose patience with me.
  中文：我担心同学们会对我的努力感到厌烦，甚至对我失去耐心。
  生词：fear v. 担心；害怕；classmate n. 同学；grow tired of 对……感到厌倦；effort n. 努力；lose patience with 对……失去耐心

screen11  第 10 句
  英文：After all, I did care about what others would think of me.
  中文：毕竟，我确实在意别人会怎么看我。
  生词：after all 毕竟；did care about 确实在乎（did 表示强调）；think of 看待；认为

screen12  第 11 句
  英文：I realized that if I didn’t stop, the entire lecture would be ruined.
  中文：我意识到，要是我不停下来，整堂课都会被毁掉。
  生词：realize v. 意识到；entire adj. 整个的；lecture n. 讲课；ruin v. 毁掉

screen13  第 12 句
  英文：“It’s okay, professor,” I shrugged (耸肩).
  中文：“没关系，教授。”我耸了耸肩。
  生词：okay adj. 没问题的；可以的；professor n. 教授；shrug v. 耸肩

screen14  第 13 句
  英文：The awkward moment ended with the class erupting into laughter.
  中文：尴尬的一幕以全班哄堂大笑告终。
  生词：awkward adj. 尴尬的；moment n. 时刻；end with 以……告终；erupt into laughter 突然爆发出笑声；哄堂大笑

screen15  第 14 句
  英文：I forced a smile, unsure how to respond further.
  中文：我勉强挤出一个笑容，不知道该再怎么回应。
  生词：force a smile 强装笑容；勉强一笑；unsure adj. 不确定的；拿不准的；respond v. 回应；回答；further adv. 进一步地

screen16  第 15 句
  英文：After that incident, I stopped acting as a “Chinese teacher.”
  中文：那件事以后，我不再充当“汉语老师”了。
  生词：incident n. 事件；事情；act as 充当；担任

screen17  第 16 句
  英文：Instead of correcting others when they were struggling to pronounce my name, I just smiled and nodded approvingly.
  中文：别人费力地念我的名字时，我不再去纠正，只是笑着点头，表示赞许。
  生词：instead of 而不是；correct v. 纠正；struggle to 费力地做；努力做；pronounce v. 念；发音；nod v. 点头；approvingly adv. 赞许地

screen18  第 17 句
  英文：This approach spared me the discomfort of having to over-explain.
  中文：这种做法让我免去了不得不过多解释的难堪。
  生词：approach n. 方法；做法；spare sb sth 使某人免受（麻烦等）；discomfort n. 难堪；不适；over-explain v. 过度解释

screen19  第 18 句
  英文：However, I soon found that by doing so, I might be losing something more important: the opportunity to share a small part of my cultural identity.
  中文：然而，我很快发现，这样做可能会让我失去某种更重要的东西：分享自己文化身份中一小部分的机会。
  生词：however adv. 然而；by doing so 这样做；opportunity n. 机会；share v. 分享；cultural adj. 文化的；identity n. 身份；认同

screen20  第 19 句
  英文：In a class discussion, I was invited to explain the meaning of my name.
  中文：在一次课堂讨论中，我受邀解释自己名字的含义。
  生词：discussion n. 讨论；invite v. 邀请；explain v. 解释；meaning n. 意思；含义

screen21  第 20 句
  英文：Many of my classmates got interested and came up to me after class.
  中文：许多同学产生了兴趣，下课后都来找我。
  生词：classmate n. 同学；get interested 产生兴趣；come up to 走到……跟前；after class 下课后
