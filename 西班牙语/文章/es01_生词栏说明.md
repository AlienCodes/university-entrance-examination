# content/ — VOCABULARY panel data (逐句精读)

- `vocab.json`: generated by `build_vocab.py` (sentence text copied from `../article.json`, never retyped).
- `validate_vocab.py`: checks that all 15 docx items appear exactly once, with the docx headword and gloss
  verbatim. It also checks that each surface is a unique, whole-word, case-exact substring of its sentence,
  that surfaces do not overlap, that n follows left-to-right surface order, that every sentence has 3–5 items,
  and that the POS legend lists exactly the tags used. Result: 9 sentences × 4 items = 36 items (15 docx + 21
  supplements), OK.
  A negative test (wrong gloss, wrong case, swapped n, overlap, unknown POS) was run against a copy and all of
  them were caught.
- POS tags follow 《现代西班牙语》/《新西汉词典》: m. f. adj. adv. tr. loc. A noun phrase takes the gender of
  its head noun (comida típica f., turismo masivo m., gente del lugar f.). A verb + preposition + infinitive
  pattern is tagged loc.
- A docx word that occurs twice (viaje: s1 and s8) is attached only at its first occurrence (s1).

## Supplementary items (one line each)
- s1 `Tengo` → tener tr. 有，怀有: irregular 1st-person form (tengo), and the frame "tener pasión por algo".
- s1 `gran` → grande adj. 大的，巨大的: apocope. grande becomes gran before a singular noun (classic grammar point).
- s2 `Me encanta` → me encanta loc. 我很喜欢: gustar-type verb with an indirect object, a stronger 喜欢.
- s3 `Cada vez que` → cada vez que loc. 每当，每次: high-frequency time connector.
- s3 `distinta` → distinto adj. 不同的: agrees in the feminine with ciudad (the translation's 陌生的 is free).
- s4 `viajar por` → viajar por loc. 在…各地旅行: por + place = travelling around/through, not "for".
- s5 `deseo` → desear tr. 希望，渴望: key verb, desear + inf.
- s5 `visitar` → visitar tr. 参观，游览: core travel verb (also in s3 "visito").
- s5 `países` → país m. 国家: the plural keeps the accent (país → países), a common spelling trap.
- s5 `en el futuro` → en el futuro loc. 将来，未来: fixed time expression.
- s6 `fastidia` → fastidiar tr. 使厌烦: "Me fastidia…" works like gustar (the thing that annoys is the subject).
- s6 `daña` → dañar tr. 损害，破坏: key verb, the sense behind 破坏 in the translation.
- s7 `Prefiero` → preferir tr. 更喜欢，宁愿: e→ie stem change, preferir + inf.
- s7 `tranquilo` → tranquilo adj. 从容的，悠闲的: adjective used predicatively ("viajar tranquilo"), not 安静的 here.
- s7 `gente del lugar` → gente del lugar f. 当地人: gente is a feminine singular collective noun (la gente es…).
  Note: "sin prisa" (不慌不忙) cannot be its own item because it would overlap the docx item "prisa" (匆忙).
  The narration/teacher can say "sin prisa = 不慌不忙" aloud.
- s8 `abierto` → abierto adj. 开朗的: context sense (a person who is open/outgoing), not the default 开着的.
  "hacer + 宾语 + adj." (使…变得) was left out to keep the panel at 4.
- s8 `enseñan a` → enseñar a + inf. loc. 教（某人）做某事: verb + a + infinitive pattern (enseñar a alguien a + inf.).
- s8 `valorar` → valorar tr. 重视，珍视: key verb (translation: 珍惜).
- s9 `nunca` → nunca adv. 从不，永不: negative adverb placed before the verb (no extra "no" needed).
- s9 `dejar de` → dejar de + inf. loc. 停止做某事: key periphrasis.
- s9 `vivir` → vivir tr. 亲身经历: transitive context sense (vivir una experiencia), not the default 生活/活着.

Left out on purpose: very transparent cognates/basics (conocer, lugar, cultura's adj. diferente, local,
observar, planeta, quiero). They were dropped to keep every panel at 4 items.

## Review decisions
Two reviewers raised two issues. After the change, `build_vocab.py` was re-run and `validate_vocab.py` reports
OK (9 × 4 = 36 items, 15 docx + 21 supplements, no warnings). The 15 docx headwords and glosses are unchanged.
- ACCEPTED: s8 n=2 `enseñar a + inf.` gloss 教会做某事 → 教（某人）做某事 (verify-chinese). The new gloss matches the
  textbook / 《新西汉词典》 pattern enseñar a alguien a + inf. It shows the person slot (the "me" in "me enseñan a"),
  avoids reading 教会 on its own as jiàohuì (church), and has 8 characters, so it fits the length limit. The
  sentence translation keeps 教会我珍惜.
- REJECTED: s2 add `probar` tr. 品尝 as a 5th item (verify-chinese, marked optional). s2 already has 3 docx items
  plus the key structure `Me encanta`, so it meets the spec's 3–4 target. The spec says "prefer 4", and 5 is only
  the ceiling. A 5th item would crowd the longest sentence (95 characters) and break the uniform 4-item panel.
  品尝 is a standard listed sense of probar, inferable from the translation's 品尝, and the narration can mention
  it. Swapping out `Me encanta` would lose a structure the spec names explicitly. probar stays the first
  candidate if a 5th slot is ever wanted.
