  英文：Online classes have become an important part of university education.
  中文：网课已经成为大学教育的重要组成部分。

  英文：In my opinion, they have both clear advantages and obvious disadvantages, and they can be improved in several ways.

  生词：in my opinion 在我看来；both…and… 既……又……；advantage n. 优点；优势；obvious adj. 明显的；disadvantage n. 缺点；不利因素；improve v. 改进；改善；in several ways 从几个方面

screen06  第 4 句
  英文：Students can attend them from anywhere, and recorded lectures allow them to review difficult points as many times as they need.
  中文：学生在任何地方都能上课，录播课程还能让他们根据需要反复复习难点。
  生词：attend v. 上（课）；出席；from anywhere 从任何地方；recorded adj. 录制的；录播的；lecture n. 讲座；讲课；allow sb. to do 让某人能够做；review v. 复习；回顾；as many times as ……多少次就多少次

screen07  第 5 句

  生词：online adj. 在线的；网络的；platform n. 平台；give sb. access to 使某人能够接触到（或使用）；excellent adj. 优秀的；极好的；course n. 课程；university n. 大学

screen08  第 6 句

  中文：另一方面，网课也有其局限性。
  生词：on the other hand 另一方面；online adj. 在线的；网络的；limit n. 局限；限度

screen09  第 7 句

  生词：face-to-face adj. 面对面的；contact n. 接触；联系；find it hard to do 觉得做……很难；concentrate v. 集中注意力；专心；tell whether… 判断是否……；lesson n. 课；一节课
