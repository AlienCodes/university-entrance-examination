screen03  第 1 句
  英文：Nowadays, many college students stay up late, skip breakfast and spend hours sitting in front of screens.
  中文：如今，许多大学生熬夜、不吃早饭，还一连几个小时坐在屏幕前。
  生词：nowadays adv. 如今；现在；college n. 大学；学院；stay up late 熬夜；skip v. 不吃（饭）；跳过；in front of 在……前面；screen n. 屏幕

screen04  第 2 句
  英文：These habits may seem harmless at first, but over time they can seriously damage students’ health.
  中文：这些习惯起初看似无害，但久而久之会严重损害学生的健康。
  生词：habit n. 习惯；harmless adj. 无害的；at first 起初；over time 久而久之；随着时间的推移；seriously adv. 严重地；damage v. 损害；伤害；health n. 健康

screen05  第 3 句
  英文：Therefore, it is important for college students to develop a healthy lifestyle.
  中文：因此，大学生养成健康的生活方式非常重要。
  生词：therefore adv. 因此；所以；it is important for sb. to do 对某人来说做……很重要；college n. 大学；学院；develop v. 养成；培养；lifestyle n. 生活方式

screen06  第 4 句
  英文：A healthy lifestyle is, first of all, the basis of a strong body.
  中文：首先，健康的生活方式是强健身体的基础。
  生词：lifestyle n. 生活方式；first of all 首先；basis n. 基础

screen07  第 5 句
  英文：Eating balanced meals, getting enough sleep and exercising regularly help students stay energetic and resist illness.
  中文：饮食均衡、睡眠充足、规律锻炼，能帮助学生保持精力充沛，抵御疾病。
  生词：balanced adj. 均衡的；平衡的；meal n. 一餐；饭食；exercise v. 锻炼；运动；regularly adv. 有规律地；定期地；energetic adj. 精力充沛的；resist v. 抵抗；抵御；illness n. 疾病

screen08  第 6 句
  英文：In addition, it benefits their studies.
  中文：此外，它对学习也有好处。
  生词：in addition 此外；benefit v. 有益于；使受益；study n. 学业；学习

screen09  第 7 句
  英文：A well-rested mind learns faster and remembers more, so students with good habits often perform better in class.
  中文：休息充分的头脑学得更快、记得更多，所以习惯良好的学生往往在课堂上表现更好。
  生词：well-rested adj. 休息充分的；habit n. 习惯；perform v. 表现；做；in class 在课堂上

screen10  第 8 句
  英文：What is more, a healthy lifestyle is good for mental health.
  中文：更重要的是，健康的生活方式有益于心理健康。
  生词：what is more 更重要的是；而且；lifestyle n. 生活方式；be good for 对……有益；mental adj. 精神的；心理的；health n. 健康

screen11  第 9 句
  英文：Regular exercise and a stable daily routine can relieve stress and keep students in a positive mood.
  中文：规律的锻炼和稳定的日常作息可以缓解压力，让学生保持积极的心情。
  生词：regular adj. 规律的；定期的；exercise n. 锻炼；运动；stable adj. 稳定的；daily adj. 日常的；routine n. 日常作息；常规；relieve v. 缓解；减轻；stress n. 压力；positive adj. 积极的；mood n. 心情；情绪

screen12  第 10 句
  英文：To develop such a lifestyle, students should keep a regular schedule, limit their screen time and take part in sports.
  中文：要养成这样的生活方式，学生应该作息规律，限制看屏幕的时间，并参加体育运动。
  生词：develop v. 养成；培养；lifestyle n. 生活方式；keep a regular schedule 作息规律；limit v. 限制；screen time 看屏幕的时间；take part in 参加；参与
