This March, fifteen international students formed a Gen Z APEC Reporters team and visited the 2026 Greater Bay Area Flower Show in Shenzhen. The event was the first youth communication activity of the APEC “China Year” in Shenzhen.

The students walked through creatively designed gardens. They took photos and shared stories about green development from a global perspective. The event aimed to show Shenzhen as a “city of parks” and spread green ideas.

A French student loved the flowers. Two Russian students admired the iris (鸢尾花), which is this year’s theme flower. One of them praised the Dapeng garden for its bright colors. A student from Turkmenistan was deeply moved by the Nanshan garden, calling it shiny and bright. A Nigerian student said that being in nature brings peace and relaxation.

Besides enjoying the views, the students also documented (记录) their visit in many ways. One Russian student wrote a blog post highlighting the City Garden of Shenzhen, while another made short videos for online sharing. The visitors said they would continue to document Shenzhen’s green development through videos and posts, presenting an eco-friendly and open city to the world.

“Gardens are small, but they carry big ideas of green development,” one member said. “We are honored to tell Shenzhen’s stories and strengthen communication between the Greater Bay Area and the world.”

The flower show ran through April 6, bringing together designers and works from APEC member economies across four sections (部分)—Master Garden, Bay Area Garden, City Garden, and Floral Market—along with eight mini-gardens, showing over 30 fantastic creations. The team will continue to visit key places of Shenzhen’s APEC-themed activities, recording the city’s development and sharing its youthful energy with the world.
