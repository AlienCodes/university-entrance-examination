Motorcycle helmets are important. They protect riders from getting hurt. In 2017, about 1,872 riders in the US were saved because of helmet use. Effective helmets do more than protect people in accidents. They also help keep wind and small flying pieces off riders’ faces.

Before the 1970s, motorcycle helmets were made of plastic, offering little protection. Today, they are made of carbon fibre (碳纤维), much lighter and stronger than before. Now, they are safer and more comfortable.

With the development of technology, modern helmets are smarter. Through Bluetooth-connected speakers, riders can make telephone calls as they travel along the road. Cameras are also found in modern helmets. They can record what is happening ahead like the cameras in cars, and they are easy to control by voice. The visors in certain helmets are able to change their colour. They become dark when the sun shines too brightly or when strong light from cars and trucks hits riders’ faces. In this way, riders can avoid strong light and see better.

These days, AI is changing people’s way of life. For motorcycle riders, AI can now be placed inside helmets to achieve greater safety. One such example is the iC-R smart helmet. This helmet can show the objects around the motorcycle. The riders don’t need to turn their heads or take their eyes off the road ahead to get a better sense of the road situation. The helmet can also receive all the important information, such as speed, direction and traffic accidents. When a rider decides where to go, the helmet shows different ways to get there on the inside of the visor. It can even warn the rider when necessary, like when a motorcycle gets too close to a car.
