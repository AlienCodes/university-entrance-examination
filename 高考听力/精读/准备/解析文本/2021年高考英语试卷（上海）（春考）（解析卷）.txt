First of all，Information Literacy is the ability to know when there is a need for information，to be able to identify，locate，evaluate，and effectively use that information for the issue or problem at hand.Secondly，it is so important for students to distinguish a real story from a fake one given the mass of information available on the web.【高分句型二】Thirdly ，Authenticity of information and Accurate check of information are not just about a technology that we should acquire ，instead learning them is about an attitude and moral accomplishment，which require us to cultivate from childhood.To sum up，we should keep pace with the times and technology to improve our ability to identify and filter information.The future era requires us not only to find numerous information ，but more importantly，to identify the truly useful information.（说明选择这两个板块的理由）
As for Website Design，Big Data Processing，they are just technology tools，and we could know about them at any time if we want to master them.（简要说明不选择这两个板块的理由）
 Yours ，
Wang Jia
