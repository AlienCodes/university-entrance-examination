screen07  第 5 句 · PART 1/2
  英文：Customers in the past nearly always paid tips after receiving a service,
  中文：过去，顾客几乎总是在接受服务之后付小费，

screen08  第 5 句 · PART 2/2
  英文：such as at the conclusion of a restaurant meal, after getting a haircut or once a pizza was delivered.
  中文：比如在餐厅用餐结束时、理完发后，或是披萨刚送到时。
