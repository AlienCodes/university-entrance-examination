## 2026年考研英语一 Part A　回信（电子邮件）

**题目**（字数要求 about 100 words；范文 105 词）

> Read the following email from your friend Paul and write him a reply.
>
> Hi Li Ming,
> I was really moved by the Chinese families’ handwritten letters you posted yesterday. They are priceless! Could you please tell me a bit more about them? And are they currently on public display somewhere? I’m very keen to see them in person. Thanks.
> Yours,
> Paul
>
> You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
> Do not use your own name in the email; use “Li Ming” instead. (10 points)

**范文**

Dear Paul,

I’m delighted that the letters moved you as much as they moved me. They were written by ordinary Chinese families over the past century: parents encouraging children studying far from home, soldiers reassuring their loved ones from the front, and couples sharing news across long distances. Faded as some of them are, they preserve genuine emotions and record the great changes of their times.

Yes, a large collection of these letters is now on public display in a family-letter museum in Beijing, and admission is free. If you come to China this summer, I’ll gladly take you there and tell you the stories behind them.

Yours,  
Li Ming

## 2026年考研英语一 Part B　图表作文（饼图 + 柱状图）

**题目**（字数要求 160-200 words；范文 196 词）

> Write an essay based on the charts below. In your essay, you should
> 1) describe the charts briefly,
> 2) interpret the charts, and
> 3) give your comments,
> Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
>
> 【图表】一项关于养老机器人的消费者接受度和首要关注点的调查
> 饼图（接受度）：完全接受 39.3%，部分接受 32.8%，不接受 27.9%
> 柱状图（首要关注点）：安全 46.30%，价格 24.90%，便利 10.70%

**范文**

The charts present the findings of a survey on consumers’ acceptance of elderly-care robots and their primary concerns. As the pie chart shows, 39.3% of the respondents fully accept these robots and another 32.8% accept them partially, while the remaining 27.9% reject them altogether. Meanwhile, the bar chart reveals that safety tops consumers’ concerns at 46.3%, far ahead of price (24.9%) and convenience (10.7%).

Several factors may account for these figures. On the one hand, as the population ages rapidly and professional caregivers are in short supply, more and more families regard robots as a practical way to care for elderly relatives, which explains why over 70% of consumers are open to them. On the other hand, elderly people are physically fragile, so even a minor malfunction could cause serious harm. Hence, safety ranks first, and some consumers reject the robots altogether.

In my view, elderly-care robots have a promising future, but their popularity depends on public trust. Manufacturers should give top priority to safety and reliability, while the government ought to set strict standards and offer subsidies to make these products affordable. Only in this way can technology truly bring comfort to our senior citizens.

## 2026年考研英语二 Part B　图表作文（柱状图）

> 1) describe and interpret the chart, and
> 2) give your comments.
> Write your answer in about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)
>
> 【图表】某项关于儿童户外活动看法的调查（柱状图）：满足好奇心 54.60%，促进观察力 54.50%，强身健体 37.20%，增强亲子互动 33.20%

**范文**

The bar chart illustrates the results of a survey on people’s views of children’s outdoor activities. As can be seen, satisfying curiosity and improving observational skills are the two most widely recognized benefits, chosen by 54.6% and 54.5% of the respondents respectively. By contrast, building physical fitness and strengthening parent-child interaction were selected by only 37.2% and 33.2% respectively.

These figures indicate that people value outdoor activities mainly for their contribution to children’s intellectual growth. Nature offers children endless things to explore, which naturally sparks their curiosity and sharpens their powers of observation. The relatively low percentages for health and family bonding, however, suggest that some benefits of outdoor activities are still underestimated.

In my opinion, physical exercise and family time outdoors are just as vital to children’s healthy development. Therefore, schools should encourage children to spend more time outdoors, and parents should join them whenever possible, so that children grow up healthy, curious and closely connected with their families.

## 2025年考研英语一 Part B　表格作文

**题目**（字数要求 160-200 words；范文 184 词）

> Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the following table. In your essay, you should
> 1) describe the table briefly,
> 2) explain its intended meaning, and
> 3) give your comments.
> You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
>
> 【表格】近年来全国居民平均每百户年末主要耐用消费品拥有量
> 年份 | 空调（台） | 洗衣机（台） | 电冰箱（台）
> 2014 | 75.2 | 83.7 | 85.5
> 2017 | 96.1 | 91.7 | 95.3
> 2020 | 117.7 | 96.7 | 101.8
> 2023 | 145.9 | 98.2 | 103.4

**范文**

The table shows the average number of air conditioners, washing machines and refrigerators owned per 100 Chinese households at the end of selected years from 2014 to 2023. The most striking change is in air conditioners, whose number nearly doubled from 75.2 to 145.9. By comparison, ownership of washing machines and refrigerators grew more gradually, from 83.7 to 98.2 and from 85.5 to 103.4 respectively.

These figures clearly reflect the steady improvement in people’s living standards. With rising incomes, durable goods that were once luxuries have become everyday necessities. Since most families already own a washing machine and a refrigerator, demand for them is approaching saturation. Air conditioners, however, are still spreading fast, because many households now install one in each room in pursuit of greater comfort.

In my view, this trend is encouraging, but it also brings new challenges. More appliances mean higher energy consumption and more electronic waste. Therefore, manufacturers should develop more energy-efficient products, and consumers should use them responsibly and recycle old ones properly. Only in this way can a better life go hand in hand with a greener planet.

## 2025年考研英语二 Part B　图表作文（柱状图）

> 1) describe and interpret the chart, and

> 【图表】某社区老年人主要日常休闲活动调查（柱状图）：看电视 90.80%，散步 68.30%，养花 34.70%，阅读 31.80%，下棋 18.40%

**范文**

Activities that demand more skill, concentration or company, such as reading and playing chess, attract far fewer people.

In my opinion, older people deserve a wider range of leisure activities. Communities could organize reading clubs, gardening classes and chess competitions, which would not only make older residents’ spare time more rewarding but also help them stay mentally active and socially connected.

## 2024年考研英语一 Part B　图画 + 图表作文

**题目**（字数要求 160-200 words；范文 184 词）

> Write an essay based on the picture and the chart below. In your essay, you should
> 1) describe the picture and the chart briefly,
> 2) interpret the implied meaning, and
> 3) give your comments.
> Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
>
> 【图画】一处标着“市民健身区”的小公园里，人们在散步、跑步、锻炼；一个男孩说：“家门口新建的小公园真不错！”
> 【图表】某市近三年公园数量（单位：座）：2020 年 406，2021 年 532，2022 年 670

**范文**

The picture and the chart reflect a pleasant change in urban life. In the picture, residents are walking, jogging and exercising happily in a public fitness area, and a boy exclaims, “The new little park near my home is wonderful!” The chart shows that the number of parks in a city rose steadily from 406 in 2020 to 532 in 2021, and then to 670 in 2022, an increase of about 65% in just two years.

The implied meaning is clear: more and more small parks are being built right on people’s doorsteps, greatly improving the quality of their daily lives. This trend results from both the government’s commitment to people-centered urban planning and citizens’ growing health awareness. These “pocket parks” offer convenient places for exercise, relaxation and social contact, making cities greener and more livable.

In my view, this practice deserves wider promotion. Planners should make full use of idle land to create more green spaces, while residents should take good care of these facilities. After all, a city truly becomes a home when everyone can enjoy nature just a few steps away.

> 1) interpret the chart, and

>
> 【图表】2012—2021 年我国居民健康素养水平（折线图）：2012 年 8.80%，2013 年 9.48%，2014 年 9.79%，2015 年 10.25%，2016 年 11.58%，2017 年 14.18%，2018 年 17.06%，2019 年 19.17%，2020 年 23.15%，2021 年 25.40%
> *健康素养（health literacy）是指个人获取和理解基本健康信息和服务，并运用这些信息和服务做出正确决策，以维护和促进自身健康的能力。健康素养水平指具备基本健康素养的人在总人群（15—69 岁城乡居民）中所占的比例。

The line chart illustrates the changes in the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. Several factors may account for this trend. First, with rising living standards, people are paying increasing attention to their health. Second, the government has made great efforts to promote health education, especially since the Healthy China 2030 plan was issued in 2016. Third, the Internet has made it much easier for people to obtain reliable health information.

In my opinion, this improvement is encouraging, yet there is still much room for progress, since about three quarters of residents still lack basic health literacy. Therefore, health knowledge should be spread more widely, especially in rural areas, so that everyone can make wise decisions about their health.

## 2021年考研英语一 Part B　图画作文

**题目**（字数要求 160-200 words；范文 190 词）

> Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the picture below. In your essay, you should
> 1) describe the picture briefly,
> 2) interpret the implied meaning, and
> 3) give your comments.
> Write your answer on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
>
> 【图画】一个穿着京剧戏服的小男孩对爸爸说：“爸爸，很多同学觉得学唱戏不好玩。”爸爸说：“你自己不是喜欢吗？那就足够了。”

**范文**

In the picture, a little boy dressed in a Peking Opera costume says to his father, “Dad, many of my classmates think learning Peking Opera is no fun.” His father smiles and replies, “You like it yourself, don’t you? Then that’s all that matters.”

The picture conveys a simple yet profound message: we should follow our own interests rather than other people’s opinions. Many young people give up their hobbies simply because others consider these hobbies dull or old-fashioned. However, it is genuine passion that keeps us going when we face difficulties and eventually leads us to success. Moreover, the boy’s love for Peking Opera reminds us that traditional art needs young successors, and only those who truly love it can carry it forward.

In my view, the father sets a good example for all parents. Parents should respect and support their children’s genuine interests rather than push them to follow the crowd. As young people, we should have the courage to stick to what we love, no matter what others say. After all, a life driven by passion is far more rewarding than one shaped by other people’s expectations.

Dear Jack,

Yours,  
Li Ming

## 2021年考研英语二 Part B　图表作文（柱状图）

**题目**（字数要求 about 150 words；范文 165 词）

> Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
> 1) interpret the chart, and
> 2) give your comments.
> You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)
>
> 【图表】某市居民体育锻炼方式调查（柱状图）：独自锻炼 54.30%，和朋友一起 47.70%，和家人一起 23.90%，团体活动 15.80%

**范文**

The bar chart illustrates how residents of a city exercise. As can be seen, exercising alone is the most popular way, chosen by 54.3% of the respondents, closely followed by exercising with friends at 47.7%. By contrast, only 23.9% exercise with their family members, and group activities rank last at a mere 15.8%.

Several factors may account for this. For one thing, exercising alone is flexible, as people can work out whenever they are free. For another, friends usually share similar interests and habits, which makes it easy for them to exercise together. Exercising with family or in groups, however, is less common, probably because of busy schedules and a lack of organized activities in communities.

In my opinion, every way of exercising has its own advantages. Whatever form people choose, what matters most is to make exercise a regular habit. In addition, communities could organize more group activities, such as weekend fun runs and family sports days, to make exercise more enjoyable and sociable.
