第39句 | 原文：It is hardly surprising that labour productivity was higher in the factory, now that the factory system gradually outperformed the putting-out system and came to replace it. | 建议：…higher in the factory, given that the factory system gradually outperformed the putting-out system and came to replace it.（或改为 as the factory system gradually outperformed …） | 理由：now that 表示“既然（现在）”，用于说话时成立的情况，与一般过去时 outperformed / came to 搭配不当；另外此处逻辑是以生产率更高解释取代，“既然取代了所以生产率更高”因果倒置，given that/as 较妥。
第54句 | 原文：homeworkers often took the day off | 建议：home-workers often took the day off | 理由：全文其余各处均写作 home-workers（第43、44、46、49、52句等），此处拼写不一致。
第32句 | 原文：Unlike today, where most people have one job, people moved… | 建议：Unlike today, when most people have one job, people (then) moved… | 理由：today 是时间，非限制性定语从句关系词应用 when 而非 where；且 Unlike today 与主语 people 比较对象不对等，严格编辑会改为 Unlike people today, most of whom have one job, people then moved…
