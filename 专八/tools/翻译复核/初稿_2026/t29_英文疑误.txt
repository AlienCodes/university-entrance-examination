第1句 | on the surface of potteries | on the surface of pottery | pottery 表“陶器”时是不可数名词；potteries 指陶器厂/陶瓷产地，不是陶器本身。
第10句 | manifest in themselves unparalleled graceful air | manifest in themselves an unparalleled graceful air | air 表“气质、神韵”时是可数用法（an air of…），单数前须加不定冠词。
第11句 | On the contrary, water-ink paintings | In contrast, water-ink paintings | on the contrary 用于否定/反驳前面的说法（“恰恰相反”），这里只是两类画法的对比，应用 in contrast / by contrast。
第21句 | reveal that Chinese think in a holistic way | reveal that the Chinese think in a holistic way | 国民统称须用 the Chinese（同 the French、the Japanese）；无冠词的 Chinese 不能作复数名词主语。
第22句 | must have an overall planning concerning | must have an overall plan concerning | planning 是不可数名词，不能与 an 连用；应为 an overall plan（或去掉 an：overall planning）。
