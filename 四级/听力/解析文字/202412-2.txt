two exercises matched up.
Of more than 9,700 people, roughly 85 percent were off base when asked to rate the quality of their diet, and
almost all overestimated how healthy it was. [4] Those who rated their diet as poor tended to be far more accurate.

Their rating match that of the researchers more than nine times out of ten.
