## 2026年6月大学英语四级 第3套　情景作文

**题目**（字数要求 at least 120 words but not more than 180 words；范文 176 词）

> Directions: Suppose it is proposed that university students should be required to participate in at least one research project. You are now to write a response by stating what you think of the proposal in terms of its necessity and practicality. You will have 30 minutes to write the response. You should write at least 120 words but not more than 180 words.

**范文**

It has been proposed that every university student should be required to take part in at least one research project. In my view, the proposal is necessary, and it is also practical as long as it is carried out flexibly.

The requirement is necessary because research teaches what ordinary classes seldom can. When students design an experiment or conduct a survey, they learn to ask good questions, analyze data and solve problems on their own. Working with teachers and teammates also develops their communication skills and sense of responsibility. These gains will benefit them whether they pursue further study or enter the job market.

The proposal is also practical. Most universities already have laboratories, libraries and teachers engaged in research, so students can join existing projects instead of starting from scratch. Projects can range from a small task in a professor’s experiment to a semester-long independent survey, allowing students of all majors to find a good fit. With proper guidance and a reasonable schedule, research will be a valuable experience for students rather than a burden.
