> Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “When faced with differing opinions, we should try to reach agreement through friendly discussion and reasonable argument.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.

**范文**

When faced with differing opinions, we should try to reach agreement through friendly discussion and reasonable argument. Whether in a dormitory, a classroom or a workplace, disagreements are unavoidable, and how we handle them often determines whether a problem is solved or made worse.

Friendly discussion creates an atmosphere of mutual respect. When people feel that their views are heard, they are more willing to listen to others instead of getting defensive. Reasonable argument, in addition, ensures that decisions are based on facts and logic rather than emotion or stubbornness. Last term, my roommates and I disagreed about when to turn off the lights at night. Instead of quarreling, we each explained our needs calmly and finally agreed on a schedule that worked for everyone. The discussion not only solved the problem but also strengthened our friendship.

To reach agreement in this way, we should keep an open mind, express our views politely and support them with evidence. We should also be willing to admit that others may be right and that we may be wrong. Ultimately, the goal of a discussion is not to win but to find the best solution together.

## 2023年6月大学英语六级 第1套　议论文（给定首句）

> Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “It is widely accepted that an important goal of education is to help students learn how to learn.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.

**范文**

It is widely accepted that an important goal of education is to help students learn how to learn. In a world where knowledge keeps growing and changing, no school can teach students everything they will need in the future.

Learning how to learn means mastering the skills that make lifelong learning possible: setting goals, finding and evaluating information, managing time and reflecting on one’s own progress. Students who have these skills can teach themselves new subjects long after they leave school. By contrast, those who are used to memorizing whatever their teachers tell them may feel helpless when facing unfamiliar problems. As the saying goes, give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach him to fish and you feed him for a lifetime.

To achieve this goal, teachers should shift from simply delivering knowledge to guiding students as they explore it, for example through projects, discussions and research tasks. Schools can also teach study skills directly, such as how to take notes and review effectively. Students, in turn, should take an active role in their own learning. Once they know how to learn, they will never stop growing.

> Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “In an era of information explosion, it is vitally important to develop the ability to think critically and make rational choices.” You can make statements, give reasons, or cite examples to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.

**范文**

In an era of information explosion, it is vitally important to develop the ability to think critically and make rational choices. Every day we are flooded with news, advertisements and opinions from countless sources, and not all of them are accurate or well-intentioned.

Critical thinking helps us separate fact from rumor. Instead of believing everything we read online, we can check where a piece of information comes from, look for evidence and compare different sources before drawing conclusions. Critical thinking also protects us from being misled by advertisements. For example, some products claim to be “scientifically proven” without offering any real evidence, and a critical thinker will question such claims instead of buying on impulse. More importantly, rational choices, whether about what to study, what to buy or whom to trust, can save us from costly mistakes and help us live wiser lives.

To develop this ability, students should get into the habit of asking questions such as “Who says so?” and “What is the evidence?” Schools can also offer courses on logic and media literacy. With a clear and critical mind, we will no longer be at the mercy of the information around us.

## 2021年12月大学英语六级 第3套　短文评论（现象 + 对策）

**题目**（字数要求 at least 150 words but no more than 200 words；范文 198 词）

> Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay related to the short passage given below. In your essay, you are to comment on the phenomenon described in the passage and suggest measures to address the issue. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.
>
> Some parents in China are overprotective of their children. They plan everything for their children, make all the decisions for them, and do not allow them to explore on their own in case they make mistakes or get hurt.

**范文**

Overprotective parenting is common in many Chinese families. Such parents arrange every detail of their children’s lives, from what to eat and wear to which university and major to choose, and they rarely let their children try anything new for fear that they might make mistakes or get hurt.

Although such behavior comes from love, it often does more harm than good. Children who have never made their own decisions may lack independence and confidence, and they may feel lost when they leave home for college or work. Moreover, mistakes and setbacks are an important part of growing up. By shielding children from every risk, parents deprive them of the chance to learn from failure and become resilient.

To address this issue, parents should first change their mindset and realize that letting go is also a form of love. They can begin by giving children age-appropriate responsibilities, such as doing chores or managing their pocket money, and allowing them to make choices and bear the consequences. Schools and communities can also help by offering parenting courses and organizing activities that encourage children to explore on their own. In this way, children will be better prepared for adult life.

>
> Young people spend a lot of time on the Internet. However, they are sometimes unable to recognize false information on the Internet, judge the reliability of online information sources, or tell real news stories from fake ones.

**范文**

Young people spend a great deal of time online, yet many of them cannot tell true information from false. They often believe sensational headlines, share unverified posts and fail to judge whether a website or an account is reliable.

This phenomenon is worrying. False information can mislead young people in their daily decisions, from what products to buy to how to deal with health problems. When they forward rumors without checking, they also help spread panic and confusion in society. The problem arises mainly because young people tend to trust whatever appears on their screens, and few of them have received training in evaluating information. In addition, some websites deliberately create eye-catching but false stories for clicks.

Several measures can help solve this problem. First, schools should offer courses on media literacy, teaching students how to check sources, compare reports and recognize the common tricks of fake news. Second, online platforms should monitor content more closely, label unverified content and remove rumors promptly. Third, young people themselves should develop the habit of thinking twice before believing or sharing anything. If everyone does their part, young people can become wise users of the Internet rather than victims of false information.

>
> Degree of urbanization in China from 1980 to 2019
>
> 【图表】折线图（城镇化率）：1980 年 19.39%，1985 年 23.71%，1990 年 26.41%，1995 年 29.04%，2000 年 36.22%，2005 年 42.99%，2010 年 49.95%，2011 年 51.27%，2013 年 53.73%，2015 年 56.1%，2016 年 57.35%，2018 年 59.58%，2019 年 60.6%（2012、2014、2017 年只画点、未标数值）

**范文**

As the graph shows, China’s urbanization rate rose steadily from 19.39% in 1980 to 60.6% in 2019. The increase was especially rapid between 1995 and 2010, when the rate jumped from 29.04% to 49.95%. In 2011, it reached 51.27%, passing the 50% mark, meaning that for the first time more Chinese people lived in urban areas than in rural areas. The rate continued to rise after that, though at a slower pace.

This remarkable progress reflects China’s great achievements in urbanization. Hundreds of millions of people have moved from the countryside to cities, where they enjoy better jobs, education and medical care. Modern infrastructure, such as subways, high-speed railways and new residential areas, has transformed the appearance of cities and greatly improved people’s quality of life. Urbanization has also served as a powerful engine of economic growth, creating a huge domestic market and countless new business opportunities.

Of course, rapid urbanization has also brought challenges, such as traffic congestion and high housing costs. Looking ahead, China should pursue people-centered urbanization, making cities greener and more livable while narrowing the gap between urban and rural areas. Then the achievements of urbanization will truly benefit everyone.
