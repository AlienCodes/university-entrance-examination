  英文：gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject.
  中文：扎实地掌握基础知识，对学生学好一门学科至关重要。

screen05  第 2 句
  英文：Just as a tall building cannot stand without a solid foundation, advanced study is impossible without a firm grasp of the fundamentals.
  中文：正如高楼没有坚实的地基就无法屹立，没有牢固掌握基础知识，深入的学习也就无从谈起。
  生词：just as 正如；solid adj. 坚实的；牢固的；foundation n. 地基；基础；advanced adj. 高级的；深入的；a firm grasp of 对……的牢固掌握；fundamentals n. 基础知识；基本原理

screen06  第 3 句

  生词：whether it is…or 无论是……还是……；mathematics n. 数学；physics n. 物理；foreign adj. 外国的；subject n. 学科；科目；be built on 建立在……之上；a set of 一套；一组；basic adj. 基本的；concept n. 概念；skill n. 技能

screen11  第 8 句 · PART 1/2
