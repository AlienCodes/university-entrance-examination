# c03 2026年6月大学英语六级 第3套 写作 Part I 增强青年责任感：第 1 屏片头（CET-6 · WRITING · PART I、标题、 试卷 · 篇目、体裁 议论文、全文 197 词 · 逐句精读），第 2 屏原题（原卷截图，静音停留到第一句开口），之后每句一屏，共 10 句；长句分屏（页脚 PART m/n）。

screen03  第 1 句
  英文：In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people’s sense of responsibility.
  中文：在社会快速发展的时代，增强年轻人的责任感至关重要。
  生词：in an era of 在……的时代；rapid adj. 快速的；迅速的；social adj. 社会的；development n. 发展；it is vitally important to 做……至关重要；enhance v. 增强；提高；a sense of responsibility 责任感

screen04  第 2 句
  英文：As the future builders of the country, young people will soon take on key roles in every field, and how responsibly they act will largely shape the society of tomorrow.
  中文：作为国家未来的建设者，年轻人很快将在各个领域担当关键角色，他们以怎样负责的态度行事，将在很大程度上塑造明天的社会。
  生词：builder n. 建设者；take on 承担；担当；key adj. 关键的；role n. 角色；作用；field n. 领域；responsibly adv. 负责地；largely adv. 在很大程度上；shape v. 塑造；影响；society n. 社会

screen05  第 3 句
  英文：A strong sense of responsibility matters on two levels.
  中文：强烈的责任感在两个层面上都很重要。
  生词：a sense of responsibility 责任感；matter v. 要紧；有重要性；on two levels 在两个层面上

screen06  第 4 句
  英文：For individuals, it is the foundation of success: students who take their studies seriously and keep their promises are far more likely to earn the trust of teachers, employers and friends.
  中文：对个人而言，责任感是成功的基础：认真对待学业、信守承诺的学生，远比别人更有可能赢得老师、雇主和朋友的信任。
  生词：individual n. 个人；foundation n. 基础；take…seriously 认真对待……；keep one's promise 信守承诺；be likely to 很可能……；earn the trust of 赢得……的信任；employer n. 雇主

screen07  第 5 句
  英文：For society, responsible young people are a major driving force behind progress.
  中文：对社会而言，有责任感的年轻人是推动进步的重要力量。
  生词：society n. 社会；responsible adj. 有责任感的；负责任的；major adj. 主要的；重要的；driving force 推动力；动力；progress n. 进步

screen08  第 6 句 · PART 1/2
  英文：In recent years, countless young volunteers have taught in rural schools, cared for elderly residents and joined environmental campaigns,
  中文：近年来，无数年轻志愿者到乡村学校支教、照顾老年居民、参加环保活动，
  生词：in recent years 近年来；countless adj. 无数的；volunteer n. 志愿者；rural adj. 乡村的；农村的；care for 照顾；照料；elderly adj. 年老的；上了年纪的；resident n. 居民；environmental adj. 环境的；环保的；campaign n. （有组织的）活动；运动

screen09  第 6 句 · PART 2/2
  英文：showing that a sense of duty can turn into concrete action.
  中文：这表明责任感能够转化为实实在在的行动。
  生词：a sense of duty 责任感；使命感；turn into 转化为；变成；concrete adj. 具体的；实在的；action n. 行动

screen10  第 7 句
  英文：Enhancing this sense of responsibility is a shared task.
  中文：增强这种责任感是一项共同的任务。
  生词：enhance v. 增强；提高；sense of responsibility 责任感；shared adj. 共同的；共有的；task n. 任务

screen11  第 8 句
  英文：Families should let children handle their own affairs and face the consequences of their choices, while schools can offer more opportunities for community service.
  中文：家庭应让孩子自己处理自己的事务，并承担自己选择带来的后果，而学校可以提供更多参与社区服务的机会。
  生词：handle v. 处理；应对；affair n. 事务；事情；face the consequences of 承担……的后果；面对……的后果；choice n. 选择；offer v. 提供；opportunity n. 机会；community service 社区服务

screen12  第 9 句
  英文：Most importantly, young people themselves should start with small things, such as finishing every task carefully and caring for those around them.
  中文：最重要的是，年轻人自身应从小事做起，比如认真完成每一项任务、关心身边的人。
  生词：most importantly 最重要的是；start with small things 从小事做起；such as 例如；比如；task n. 任务；carefully adv. 认真地；仔细地；care for 关心；关爱

screen13  第 10 句
  英文：Only in this way can they shoulder the responsibilities of their time.
  中文：只有这样，他们才能担负起时代赋予的责任。
  生词：only in this way 只有这样（后面的句子要部分倒装）；shoulder v. 承担；担负；responsibility n. 责任；time n. 时代
