# c05 2025年12月大学英语六级 第2套 写作 Part I 教师对学生的深远影响：第 1 屏片头（CET-6 · WRITING · PART I、标题、 试卷 · 篇目、体裁 议论文、全文 196 词 · 逐句精读），第 2 屏原题（原卷截图，静音停留到第一句开口），之后每句一屏，共 10 句；长句分屏（页脚 PART m/n）。

screen03  第 1 句
  英文：It is believed that teachers can exert a profound influence on their students’ academic pursuit and personal development.
  中文：人们认为，教师能够对学生的学业追求和个人发展产生深远的影响。
  生词：it is believed that 人们认为……；exert a profound influence on 对……产生深远的影响；academic adj. 学业的；学术的；pursuit n. 追求；personal adj. 个人的；development n. 发展

screen04  第 2 句
  英文：Beyond passing on knowledge, a good teacher can shape how students think, what they value and even which path they choose in life.
  中文：好老师除了传授知识，还能塑造学生的思维方式、价值取向，甚至影响他们人生道路的选择。
  生词：beyond prep. 除了……之外；pass on 传授；传递；knowledge n. 知识；shape v. 塑造；影响；value v. 重视；看重；path n. 道路

screen05  第 3 句
  英文：Academically, teachers act as guides who spark curiosity.
  中文：在学业上，教师是激发好奇心的引路人。
  生词：academically adv. 在学业上；act as 充当；担任；guide n. 引路人；向导；spark v. 激发；引发；curiosity n. 好奇心

screen06  第 4 句
  英文：An inspiring lecture or a single word of encouragement may awaken a lifelong interest in a subject.
  中文：一堂鼓舞人心的讲座或一句鼓励的话，就可能唤起人对某门学科终身的兴趣。
  生词：inspiring adj. 鼓舞人心的；启发灵感的；lecture n. 讲座；讲课；a single word of encouragement 一句鼓励的话；awaken v. 唤起；激起；lifelong adj. 终身的；毕生的；interest in 对……的兴趣；subject n. 学科；科目

screen07  第 5 句
  英文：My high school physics teacher, for example, often used simple experiments to explain difficult concepts, and it was in her class that I first dreamed of becoming an engineer, a dream I am still pursuing today.
  中文：比如，我的高中物理老师常常用简单的实验来讲解难懂的概念，正是在她的课上，我第一次萌生了当工程师的梦想，这个梦想我至今仍在追求。
  生词：physics n. 物理；for example 例如；experiment n. 实验；explain v. 讲解；解释；concept n. 概念；it was…that 正是……（强调句型）；engineer n. 工程师；pursue v. 追求

screen08  第 6 句
  英文：In terms of personal development, teachers serve as role models.
  中文：在个人发展方面，教师是学生的榜样。
  生词：in terms of 在……方面；就……而言；personal adj. 个人的；development n. 发展；serve as 充当；作为；role model 榜样

screen09  第 7 句
  英文：Their honesty, patience and sense of responsibility quietly teach students how to treat others and how to cope with difficulties.
  中文：他们的诚实、耐心和责任感，会潜移默化地教会学生如何待人、如何应对困难。
  生词：honesty n. 诚实；patience n. 耐心；sense of responsibility 责任感；quietly adv. 默默地；悄悄地；treat others 待人；对待他人；cope with 应对；处理；difficulty n. 困难

screen10  第 8 句
  英文：Given such influence, teachers should not only improve their teaching skills but also care about the growth of every student, especially those who are struggling.
  中文：鉴于这样的影响，教师不仅应该提高自己的教学技能，还应关心每一个学生的成长，尤其是那些学习吃力的学生。
  生词：given prep. 鉴于；考虑到；influence n. 影响；not only…but also 不仅……而且……；improve v. 提高；改进；teaching skills 教学技能；care about 关心；growth n. 成长；especially adv. 尤其；特别；struggle v. （学习）吃力；处境困难

screen11  第 9 句
  英文：Students, in turn, should make the most of every chance to ask questions and learn from their teachers, both inside and outside the classroom.
  中文：学生则应充分利用每一次机会提问、向老师学习，无论在课堂内外。
  生词：in turn 反过来；相应地；make the most of 充分利用；ask questions 提问；learn from 向……学习；both inside and outside the classroom 课堂内外

screen12  第 10 句
  英文：When teachers and students respect and inspire each other, education can truly fulfill its purpose.
  中文：当师生相互尊重、相互激励时，教育才能真正实现它的目的。
  生词：respect v. 尊重；inspire v. 激励；鼓舞；each other 互相；education n. 教育；truly adv. 真正地；fulfill v. 实现；履行；purpose n. 目的；宗旨
