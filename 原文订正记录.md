## 2024 全国新课标Ⅱ卷 阅读C

- 原文：farmer’s markets
  订正：farmers’ markets
  理由：The markets belong to many farmers, not one, so the singular possessive is wrong. Use the plural possessive “farmers’ markets” (or “farmers markets” in AP style).
- 原文：in the same building where it’s eaten
  订正：in the same building where the food is eaten
  理由：“it” has no proper antecedent. The only singular noun before it is “a system”, so the sentence literally says the system is eaten. Naming the food makes the reference clear.
- 原文：By connecting through the Cloud, BMF is remotely monitored.
  订正：Connected through the Cloud, BMF is remotely monitored.
  理由：The by-gerund clashes with the passive main clause. Who does the “connecting” is unclear: the unnamed people doing the monitoring, or BMF? A past-participle modifier attaches cleanly to the subject BMF.
- 原文：most produce at the store went through weeks of travel and covered hundreds of miles before reaching the table
  订正：most produce at the store goes through weeks of travel and covers hundreds of miles before reaching the table
  理由：This states a general fact in a present-tense passage ("We all know fresh is best"), so the simple past "went/covered" is wrong here. Also, produce still at the store has not yet reached the table. The habitual present fixes both problems.
